Why Not Just Build It Yourself?

Stitching open-source tools together is rarely free.

You can wire up a portfolio spreadsheet, an open-source Kanban tool, a community helpdesk, and an asset tracker — but then you own every integration between them. When one tool updates its API, your traceability breaks. The Wayfarer Suite is built as one platform: the initiative_id chain is maintained by the products themselves, not by a brittle script someone has to babysit. You get purpose-built integration without the maintenance bill.
