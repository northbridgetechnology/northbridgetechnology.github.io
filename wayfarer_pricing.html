Common Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Is support required?

No. Support is completely optional on every product and bundle. Add it any time if your needs change.

Can I mix standalone and suite products?

Yes. Start with one product and add others later — suite add-on rates apply once you qualify. Contact us and we'll structure it.

Are there really no per-seat fees?

Correct. Your license price is fixed within its tier no matter how many people use it.

What if my team grows past the Waystation tier?

You simply move to the next agent tier. Every other product is headcount-independent.

Do suite bundles include support?

No — support is always a separate optional add-on, so you only pay for what you need.

Can we try before we buy?

Absolutely. Request a demo and we'll walk you through a live environment.
